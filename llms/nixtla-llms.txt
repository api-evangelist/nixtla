# Nixtla — AI-Powered Time Series Forecasting & Anomaly Detection
> https://www.nixtla.io

Nixtla is an enterprise-grade time series intelligence platform. We provide TimeGPT, the first foundation model for time series, enabling zero-shot forecasting, anomaly detection, and demand prediction without training data. Trusted by Microsoft, Lyft, Prudential, Decathlon, and leading enterprises worldwide.

## Products

### TimeGPT
The first foundation model for time series forecasting. Zero-shot predictions, anomaly detection, and demand forecasting via a simple API. No training data required.
- Zero-shot forecasting (no training needed)
- Anomaly detection at scale
- Multi-horizon predictions with conformal intervals
- Exogenous variables support
- Fine-tuning on custom data
- SOC 2 Type II compliant
https://www.nixtla.io/

### TimeGPT-2
Next generation foundation model with improved accuracy and expanded capabilities for time series analysis.
https://www.nixtla.io/timegpt-2-waitlist

### Open Source Ecosystem (49M+ downloads)
- **StatsForecast**: Lightning-fast statistical forecasting (AutoARIMA, ETS, CES, MSTL, Theta)
- **NeuralForecast**: Deep learning models (NBEATS, NHITS, PatchTST, TimesNet, TFT)
- **MLForecast**: Machine learning forecasting (LightGBM, XGBoost, scikit-learn)
- **HierarchicalForecast**: Hierarchical reconciliation methods
- **CoreForecast**: High-performance C/C++ backend
- **UtilsForecast**: Common utilities for time series processing
https://github.com/Nixtla
